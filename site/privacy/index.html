Privacy

This site tells me when someone reads it, so I know whether the portfolio is being looked at. It does this without cookies and without building a profile of you.

What is recorded

  • That a visit happened, and when.
  • The site you came from, if your browser shares it (for example linkedin.com).
  • Whether you are on a phone or a computer.
  • Your approximate city and country, and the name of the network you connect through, as Cloudflare resolves them. On a company network this can be the company's name.
  • Which sections you scrolled to, how long the page was in view, and which links you followed, including a CV download.
  • A label, if you arrived through a link I sent that carries one (?r=…).

What is not recorded

  • No cookies and no tracking across sites. A random tag in your browser's session storage groups one visit's events and disappears when you close the tab.
  • Your IP address and browser details are not forwarded or kept by me.
  • Nothing is sold, shared for advertising, or combined with other data.

Where it goes

Each event passes through a Cloudflare Worker and arrives as a short message in my private Telegram chat. There is no database. Cloudflare and Telegram process the request to deliver it, and the site itself is served by GitHub Pages, which keeps its own server logs.

Opting out

If your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, nothing is recorded. Blocking JavaScript has the same effect; the site works without it.

Questions

The basis for this is my legitimate interest in knowing whether my portfolio is read. To ask about it or have messages about your visit deleted, write to ozgur.cetintas@yahoo.com.
