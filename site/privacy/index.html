Privacy

To know whether my portfolio is read, this site tells me about visits: when they happen, the referring site, phone or computer, approximate city and country, the sections viewed and links followed, and the ?r= label of a link I sent. No cookies, and no IP address, network or browser details are stored.

To count unique visitors per day, a one-way hash of your IP address and browser is made with a key that changes daily and kept for two days at most; it cannot be turned back into either, and only the daily totals remain.

Each visit reaches me as a message in my private Telegram chat via Cloudflare, and messages are deleted automatically after 24 hours.

The basis is my legitimate interest in knowing whether the portfolio is read. If your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, nothing is recorded.
